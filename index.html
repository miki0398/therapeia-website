About Therapeia

Therapeia is built on the Neurological Functional Baseline (NFB), a proprietary precision medicine AI-assisted intra-individual longitudinal monitoring system. Unlike population-norm comparisons, NFB detects undetected deviations within each patient's own baseline—capturing the earliest signs of neurological change.

Disease-Agnostic Design

Designed for glioblastoma (GBM) pilot; expandable to Parkinson's, Alzheimer's, and beyond.

Integrated Ecosystem

Pythia Field (patient app), Pythia Navigator (clinician intelligence), and Therapeia Platform (federated governance).

Zero False Alarm Standard

Architected to the ZFA standard to minimize unnecessary alerts while maximizing clinical sensitivity.

Regulatory-Ready

HIPAA-compliant and FHIR R4 interoperable for seamless EHR integration.
