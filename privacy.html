Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Therapeia Inc. ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our website and through our services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our platform.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect on our site includes:

  • Personal Data: Name, email address, phone number, organization affiliation
  • Health Information: Functional assessment data, symptom tracking, clinical history (with explicit consent)
  • Device Information: Device type, IP address, browser type, operating system
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, interactions with the platform

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • To send administrative information and updates
  • To respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • To conduct neurological monitoring and clinical analysis (with consent)
  • To comply with legal obligations and regulations (HIPAA, GDPR, etc.)
  • To prevent fraudulent transactions and unauthorized access
  • To send marketing communications (only with your consent)

4. HIPAA Compliance

Therapeia is committed to complying with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). We implement appropriate administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect health information from unauthorized access, use, and disclosure. All personal health information (PHI) is encrypted in transit and at rest.

5. Data Security

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your personal and health information, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Role-based access controls and authentication
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Secure API endpoints with OAuth 2.0 authentication
  • FHIR R4 compliance for interoperability

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your data at any time, subject to applicable legal requirements. Deletion of active accounts will be processed within 30 days.

7. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with:

  • Healthcare providers and institutions (with explicit consent)
  • Business partners and service providers under data processing agreements
  • Law enforcement, when required by law or court order
  • Research partners (only with explicit consent and anonymization)

8. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (right to be forgotten)
  • Opt-Out: Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Data Portability: Request your data in a portable format

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@therapeia.ai
  • Mailing Address: Therapeia Inc., [Address]
  • Phone: [Phone Number]

10. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

11. GDPR and International Compliance

If you are a resident of the European Union or other jurisdiction with data protection regulations, we comply with applicable laws including GDPR. Your data is processed lawfully and with appropriate safeguards.
